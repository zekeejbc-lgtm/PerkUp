# Personal Data Breach Response

1. Anyone discovering a suspected incident immediately informs the DPO and technical incident lead.
2. Preserve logs without copying unnecessary personal data. Record discovery time, systems, data categories, affected people, containment, and evidence custody.
3. Revoke credentials/sessions, isolate affected integrations, block abusive traffic, and preserve service availability where safe.
4. Within the first 24 hours, assess unauthorized acquisition, sensitive or identity-fraud-enabling data, affected count, likely harm, and processor involvement.
5. The DPO decides and documents whether notification is required. For a qualifying breach, notify the National Privacy Commission and affected data subjects within 72 hours of knowledge or reasonable belief, using currently available facts. Do not delay the initial notice merely because the investigation continues.
6. Notifications describe the nature of the breach, likely consequences, affected data, containment, protective steps for users, and DPO contact.
7. Maintain an incident and breach register, post-incident review, corrective-action owners, deadlines, and evidence of completion.

Emergency contacts to complete: DPO name/mobile, incident lead, executive decision-maker, legal counsel, Supabase, Google, Vercel, and PayMongo escalation channels.

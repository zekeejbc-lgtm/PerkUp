# Data Subject Request Procedure

Requests are accepted at perkup.shop@youthserviceph.org with subject `Perk Privacy Request`.

1. Log the request and acknowledge receipt without exposing account existence to an unverified requester.
2. Verify identity proportionately using the registered channel; never request a password or full payment credential.
3. Classify the request: information/access, correction, portability, objection/restriction, consent withdrawal, erasure/blocking, complaint, or human review.
4. Search Auth, application tables, managed Drive files, feedback, billing, logs, and relevant processors. Exclude another person’s data and protect privileged/security information.
5. Export portable data in structured JSON or CSV. Explain sources, purposes, recipients, last modification, retention, and automated checks.
6. Correct data and notify recipients where required. For deletion, use self-service deletion when available or the assisted deletion workflow.
7. Record the decision, legal basis for any refusal or retention, actions, recipients notified, and completion date. Escalate overdue or disputed requests to the DPO.

Internal target: acknowledge within 3 business days and complete within 30 calendar days unless counsel documents a different lawful period.
